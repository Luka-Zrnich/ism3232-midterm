## This program allows us to see who paid, what they paid, if they need to make a prepayment, and if they need a payment
## The bean cost is price times bags, >20 bags gets no discount, 20-79 gets 9 percent, 80+ gets 14% and partners get an ectra 4 percent of discount, 35 dollar fee is added when the discounted cost is below 250, shipment total of 1500 or more requires prepayment, and a shipment needs a pallet when it has more than 60 bags and shipment total is over 700
